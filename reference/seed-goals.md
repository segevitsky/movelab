# Seed goals (draft — owner to edit)

Each goal: ordered stages → pass criterion → slot it occupies in a workout.

## Pull-ups (target 15–20 clean)  · slot: strength, early in round
1. Active hang + scapular pulls — 3 × 30 s hang, 3 × 10 scap pulls
2. Negatives (5 s lowering) — 3 × 5
3. Band-assisted pull-ups — 3 × 8
4. Sets of 3–5 strict — 5 × 3
5. Volume to 10 — 3 × 10
6. 15–20 strict or weighted pull-ups

## Muscle-up  · slot: skill, first in round
Entry: 10 strict pull-ups + 10 straight-bar dips
1. High pull-ups (chest to bar) — 3 × 5
2. Explosive pull-ups — 3 × 5
3. Low-bar transition drills with feet — 3 × 5
4. Band-assisted muscle-up — 3 × 3
5. Strict muscle-up — 1 clean rep, then 3 × 3

## L-sit  · slot: skill/core
1. Straight-arm support (shoulders depressed) — 3 × 30 s
2. Seated leg lifts (hands by knees) — 3 × 10
3. Tuck L-sit — 3 × 20 s
4. One-leg L-sit, alternating — 3 × 10 s per leg
5. Full L-sit — 3 × 10 s → build to 20 s

## Front splits  · slot: warm-up + cool-down, near daily
1. Low lunge hip-flexor stretch — 2 × 60 s per side
2. Half splits (hamstring) — 2 × 60 s per side
3. Active lifts (leg off floor in half split) — 2 × 10 per side
4. Splits on blocks — 2 × 60 s per side
5. Full front split

## Knee strength  · slot: legs
1. Backward walking — 3 × 30 m
2. Tibialis raises — 3 × 20
3. Spanish squat (band) — 3 × 30 s
4. Poliquin step-up — 3 × 12 per side
5. Deep split squat — 3 × 8 per side
6. Pistol squat — 3 × 5 per side

## Crawls  · slot: warm-up
1. Bear crawl — 3 × 20 m
2. Crab walk — 3 × 20 m
3. Lizard crawl — 3 × 10 m
4. Transitions between crawls — 3 × 60 s
5. Continuous 60 s flow
